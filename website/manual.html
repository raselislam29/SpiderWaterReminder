Manual & FAQs

SpiderWaterReminder is a free Windows app by Rasel Islam.

How it works

1. Setup: Install, open SpiderWaterReminder from the Start Menu, then click the tray icon near the clock.

2. Set the time: Type your reminder text and pick At Time or Repeat.

3. The drop: At the set time, the reminder character drops from the top of every screen with your message.

Controls

Esc Dismiss the drop instantly.

Click Click anywhere on the overlay to close it.

FAQs

Is it free?

Yes — free forever, no ads.

Who built it?

Can reminders repeat?

Yes. Use Repeat mode and choose every 15 / 30 / 45 minutes or every hour.
